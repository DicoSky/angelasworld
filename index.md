# Privacy Policy for Angela's World

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026

Welcome to **Angela's World** ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

By installing and playing Angela's World, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
* **Account Information:** If you choose to sign in using **Google Sign-In**, we receive your basic public profile information (such as your display name and email address) provided by Google.
* **Guest Mode:** You can play Angela's World as a guest without creating an account or providing any personal information. In guest mode, your game progress is stored locally on your device.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
* **Game Progress & Scores:** If signed in, we store your game records, high scores, flowers collected, stages reached, and game preferences in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) to sync your progress across devices.
* **Device & Usage Information:** We and our third-party partners may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device data, including:
  * Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  * Android Advertising ID (AAID) for advertising and analytics purposes.
  * App crashes and performance data.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services We Use

Our App integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device. Below are the third parties and links to their privacy policies:

* **Google Play Services**  
  Used for authentication, app distribution, and platform features.  
  [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

* **Google AdMob**  
  Used to display banner ads and rewarded video advertisements (e.g., watching an ad to revive your character). AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.  
  [Google AdMob & Privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

* **Firebase (Google LLC)**  
  * **Firebase Authentication:** To authenticate users securely with Google Sign-In.
  * **Cloud Firestore:** To store and synchronize your game scores and profile data securely in the cloud.  
  [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
* Provide, maintain, and improve gameplay features and mechanics.
* Synchronize your high scores and game progress across sessions and devices.
* Display relevant banner and rewarded advertisements.
* Monitor performance, diagnose crashes, and protect against fraudulent activity.

---

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

* **Rewarded Ads:** Players may optionally choose to watch video ads provided by Google AdMob in exchange for in-game revives.
* **Opting Out of Personalized Ads:** You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under **Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID** or **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Data Retention:** We retain your account data and gameplay history for as long as your account remains active.
* **Account & Data Deletion:** If you wish to delete your account and associated gameplay records from our database, you can contact us at the support email provided below. We will delete your records within 30 days of receiving your verified request.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Angela's World is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us so that we can promptly remove the information.

---

## 7. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Network transmissions to Firebase and AdMob are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS). While we strive to protect your information, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
